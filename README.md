## 🤖 AI & Automation Transparency

This project utilizes artificial intelligence as part of an automated content aggregation and editorial pipeline:
- **Research:** Live news context and market trends are fetched via Google News RSS.
- **Drafting & Synthesis:** Articles and structured Markdown summaries are generated using the **Google Gemini API** (`google-genai`).
- **Monetization:** Product comparison banners and affiliate search links are algorithmically injected based on the article topic.
- **Compliance:** All generated articles include mandatory affiliate disclosure disclaimers in accordance with FTC guidelines.
